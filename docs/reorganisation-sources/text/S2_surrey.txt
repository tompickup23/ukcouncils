Timeline 5 February 2025 The Minister of State for Local Government and English Devolution wrote to all councils in Surrey, enclosing an invitation to submit a proposal for unitary local government. Councils in Surrey are working to an accelerated timeline for local government reorganisation as set out in their invitation letter . 17 June 2025 A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament. This set out that a consultation on proposals for local government reorganisation in Surrey had been launched. 28 October 2025 A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament. This set out the Secretary of State’s decision to implement the proposal for 2 unitary councils in Surrey. Accompanying this announcement, letters were sent to: Surrey council leaders Surrey council chief executives Commissioners at Spelthorne Borough Council and Woking Borough Council 14 January 2026 Draft secondary legislation has been laid before Parliament, to abolish existing councils, establish new councils and make transitional arrangements. This is now subject to Parliamentary approval. 9 March 2026 The Minister of State for Local Government and Homelessness signed the Surrey (Structural Changes) Order 2026 , implementing the decision of the Secretary of State to abolish the existing district and county councils in Surrey and create 2 new councils, East Surrey Council and West Surrey Council, from 1 April 2027. 20 May 2026 The Minister of State for Local Government and Homelessness wrote to the newly elected leaders of the new East Surrey Council and West Surrey Council, congratulating them on their elections and setting out the next steps to ensure the new councils are in place to go live on 1 April 2027. 7 July 2026 A direction has been issued to councils in Surrey relating to financial transactions during local government reorganisation. This will come into effect on 8 July 2026. A letter to the Surrey councils’ chief executives sets out the direction and accompanying guidance note.
