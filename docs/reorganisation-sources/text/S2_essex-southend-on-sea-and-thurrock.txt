Timeline 5 February 2025 The invitation letter sent to councils in ​Essex, Southend-on-Sea and Thurrock set out their milestones for reorganisation. They were invited to submit final proposals for unitary local government by 26 September 2025. 19 November 2025 A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament. This set out that a statutory consultation on proposals for local government reorganisation in Essex, Southend-on-Sea and Thurrock was published. The Minister of State for Local Government and Homelessness also wrote to the leaders of councils in Essex, Southend-on-Sea and Thurrock to thank them for their proposals and inform them of the consultation. 25 March 2026 A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament, setting out the Secretary of State’s decision to implement the proposal for 5 unitary councils to be established in Essex. Secondary legislation, which will be subject to Parliamentary approval, will now be taken forward to abolish existing councils, establish new councils and make transitional arrangements. ​Accompanying this announcement, letters were sent to: Essex, Southend-on-Sea and Thurrock council leaders 7 September 2026 On 7 September 2026, the Minister for Local Government, Devolution and Regional Growth wrote to local government reorganisation council leaders. This letter announced the withdrawal of the current decisions for: Essex, Southend-on-Sea and Thurrock Hampshire, Isle of Wight, Portsmouth and Southampton Norfolk Suffolk It also announced a rapid review of the local government reorganisation programme. This includes decisions taken in July 2026 and the 2 outstanding areas awaiting decisions. The Secretary of State also announced these measures in an oral statement to Parliament on 7 September 2026.
