Timeline 5 February 2025 The invitation letter sent to councils in Cambridge and Peterborough set out their milestones for reorganisation. They were invited to submit final proposals for unitary local government by 28 November 2025. 5 February 2026 ​A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament. This set out that a statutory consultation on the proposals received for local government reorganisation in Cambridge and Peterborough was launched. On the same day, the Minister of State for Local Government and Homelessness, Alison McGovern MP, wrote to the leaders of councils in Cambridge and Peterborough to thank them for their proposals and to inform them of the consultation. 16 July 2026 A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament that provided an update on local government reorganisation in Cambridgeshire and Peterborough. Leaders in Cambridgeshire and Peterborough also received a letter from the Secretary of State .
