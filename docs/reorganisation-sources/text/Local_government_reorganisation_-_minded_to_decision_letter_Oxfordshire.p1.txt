Jim McMahon OBE MP Minister for Local Government, Devolution and Regional Growth 2 Marsham Street London SW1P 4DF Council Leaders in Oxfordshire and West Berkshire By email 29 October 2026 Dear Leaders, I am writing to set out the outcome of the Government’s review of the framework for the local government reorganisation programme and, separately, the Secretary of State’s current provisional view on the proposals for local government reorganisation in Oxfordshire. We are grateful for all the work that went into developing proposals and preparing for implementation, and for the patience you have shown throughout this process. We recognise that the review period has not been easy, and do not take for granted your continued commitment to the long-term work needed to fix the foundations and put local government in the best possible position. Outcome of the programme review Following the announcement to Parliament on 7 September 2026, the Secretary of State and I have worked with officials to interrogate the key pillars and framework underpinning the Local Government Reorganisation programme, including asking whether the existing unweighted criteria (‘criteria’) set in February 2025 and its objectives continue to support the policy priorities of the new Government. We have concluded that the basis of the programme is robust and that the criteria against which proposals have been submitted were both correct on their own terms and continue to support the Government’s policy agenda. Following this initial phase of the review, we can therefore confirm that the Government remains committed to delivering Local Government Reorganisation and that we will continue to move forward with the programme based on the criteria, which we hope gives confidence as the programme progresses. Minded to decision for Oxfordshire Separately from the programme-level review, the Secretary of State and I, in my capacity as Minister for Local Government, Devolution and Regional Growth, are currently minded to implement the three unitary option submitted by Oxford City Council with modifications to the proposed boundaries to create Greater Oxford Council, Northern Oxfordshire Council and Ridgeway Council. Elections to new councils would take place in May 2028, ahead of vesting in April 2029.
