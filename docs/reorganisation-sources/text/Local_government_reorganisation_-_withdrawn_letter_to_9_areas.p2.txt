Decisions Separately from the programme-level review, together with the Secretary of State, I have been carefully considering all the proposals received, the merits of these proposals, and all relevant evidence and views against the existing criteria. I can confirm that we will issue a number of minded to decisions, with a programme of further decision-making, which I outline in further detail below. We have concluded that further time is required to consider the outcome in your nine invitation areas. We will undertake further consideration in the next phase of the review for your areas. To give you clarity on the status of the existing decisions while I do that, we have decided to today withdraw the decisions announced in July 2026 for your area. That means decisions from July 2026 have been withdrawn for the following nine areas: • • • • • • • • • Derbyshire and Derby Devon, Plymouth and Torbay Hertfordshire Kent and Medway Lancashire, Blackburn with Darwen, and Blackpool Leicestershire, Leicester and Rutland Lincolnshire, North Lincolnshire and North East Lincolnshire Nottinghamshire and Nottingham Staffordshire and Stoke-on-Trent This is not a reflection of the quality of the proposals submitted by councils in your area, but rather the need to give each decision of such significance the careful consideration it deserves. I want to be clear that the review remains ongoing, and we will thoroughly consider all the evidence and analysis available to us, before coming to a view on next steps for your area. For the avoidance of doubt, all options and proposals in your area will remain under consideration as part of the review. We have also set out today that we have made “minded to” decisions in the five remaining areas that received decisions in July 2026. Before we take final decisions for these areas, there will be a short period of time during which those interested may make further representations to me. More detail on these areas can be found in the Written Statement I will make to Parliament today. Next phases of the review: timetable and next steps I now turn to the next phase of the review, which will focus on the following areas due to have mindedto decisions, in no particular order: • • • The nine areas listed above where decisions have now been withdrawn; The four areas (Essex, Southend-on-Sea and Thurrock; Hampshire, Isle of Wight, Portsmouth and Southampton; Norfolk; and Suffolk) where decisions were withdrawn in September; and The two areas (Cambridgeshire and Peterborough; and West Sussex) where decisions have not yet been taken. I am mindful that you want clarity and I will work as fast as I can with officials to provide that clarity. As I have said, I believe it is right to take a structured approach to the review and give each area the serious and deep consideration it deserves given the significance of these decisions. We will, however, seek to inform councils at the earliest practical opportunity, meaning that some of these 15 remaining areas will receive an outcome before others. We commit to providing updates on outcomes in another set of areas by Christmas – and that every area will have an outcome announced by early next year.
