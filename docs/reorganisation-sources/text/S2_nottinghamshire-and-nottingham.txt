Timeline 5 February 2025 The invitation letter sent to councils in Nottinghamshire and Nottingham set out their milestones for reorganisation. They were invited to submit final proposals for unitary local government by 28 November 2025. 5 February 2026 ​A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament. This set out that a statutory consultation on the proposals received for local government reorganisation in Nottinghamshire and Nottingham was launched. On the same day, the Minister of State for Local Government and Homelessness, Alison McGovern MP, wrote to the leaders of councils in Nottinghamshire and Nottingham to thank them for their proposals and to inform them of the consultation. Ashfield District Council and Broxtowe Borough Council did not submit proposals for unitary local government and so the letter is not addressed to their leaders. 16 July 2026 ​A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament, setting out the Secretary of State’s decision to implement the proposal for 2 unitary councils to be established in Nottinghamshire and Nottingham. ​Secondary legislation, which will be subject to Parliamentary approval, will now be taken forward to abolish existing councils, establish new councils and make transitional arrangements. ​Accompanying this announcement, letters were sent to leaders in Nottinghamshire and Nottingham .
