Timeline 5 February 2025 The invitation letter sent to West Sussex councils set out their milestones for reorganisation. They were invited to submit final proposals by 26 September 2025. 19 November 2025 A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament. This set out that a consultation on local government reorganisation in West Sussex was launched. ​For the purposes of the consultation the proposals in West Sussex and East Sussex and Brighton and Hove have been grouped together. On the same day, the Minister of State for Local Government and Homelessness, Alison McGovern MP, wrote to the leaders of councils in West Sussex to thank them for their proposals and to inform them of the consultation. 25 March 2026 A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament, setting out that the Secretary of State has not yet made a decision about the proposals received from West Sussex. The Secretary of State wrote to West Sussex council leaders to explain the rationale for this, noting that he is considering modifications to the proposals that may address his concerns. Accompanying this announcement, a letter was sent to West Sussex council leaders . 27 March 2026 MHCLG officials wrote to West Sussex council chief executives about the next steps for a consultation on potential modifications to the 2 unitary proposal submitted by West Sussex District and Borough councils. 12 May 2026 ​Alison McGovern MP, Minister for Local Government and Homelessness, wrote to the West Sussex council leaders to provide information on a further consultation being launched. This consultation seeks views on the options for potential modification to the proposal. 16 July 2026 A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament that provided an update on local government reorganisation in West Sussex. Leaders in West Sussex also received a letter from the Secretary of State .
