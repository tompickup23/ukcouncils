The previous decision The Secretary of State has today decided to withdraw the decision announced on 16 July 2026 concerning local government reorganisation in Oxfordshire. That earlier decision is not being treated as determining the outcome of the present reconsideration. The Secretary of State is considering the proposals afresh and will reach a new final decision after considering the representations invited by this letter and any other relevant matters. Statutory process The Local Government and Public Involvement in Health Act 2007 provides the statutory framework for local government structural change. The statutory process permits the Secretary of State to implement a proposal with or without modification following the required consultation. If, following the further consideration described in this letter, the Secretary of State decides to implement a proposal, the structural changes would need to be provided for in a structural changes order under section 7 of the 2007 Act, subject to the applicable Parliamentary procedure. This letter does not itself establish any new council, alter any existing local government boundary or otherwise implement structural change. Capacity funding and other matters I recognise that councils have already incurred costs associated with preparing for local government reorganisation and have committed significant time, resource and leadership to this work. I am pleased to confirm that Oxfordshire will be paid 50% of the £900,000 per new unitary transition support funding. This is equal to £1.35 million. Grants will be paid in November to these previously confirmed recipient authorities. When final decisions are made, we will pay any remaining balance of the £900,000 transition support per each new unitary, taking account of funding already paid to its area, as well as £150,000 of supplementary funding per new unitary for Leadership Capacity and Continuity in Children’s Services, Adult Social Care and Public Health. We are setting out further detail in the Written Ministerial Statement on city status and ceremonial matters, and the transition from the committee system.
