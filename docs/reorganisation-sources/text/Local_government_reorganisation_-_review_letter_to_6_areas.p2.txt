Decisions Separately from the programme-level review, together with the Secretary of State, I have been carefully considering all the proposals received, the merits of these proposals, and all relevant evidence and views against the existing criteria. I can confirm that we will issue a number of minded to decisions, with a programme of further decision-making, which I outline in further detail below. We can confirm that no new decisions have been taken in your areas so far. We have concluded that further time is required to undertake further consideration in the next phase of the review for your areas. This is not a reflection of the quality of the proposals submitted by councils in your area, but rather the need to give each decision of such significance the careful consideration it deserves. I want to be clear that the review remains ongoing, and we will thoroughly consider all the evidence and analysis available to us, before coming to a view on next steps for your area. For the avoidance of doubt, all options and proposals in your area will remain under consideration as part of the review. We have also set out today that we have made “minded to” decisions in five areas that received decisions in July 2026. Before we take final decisions for these areas, there will be a short period of time during which those interested may make further representations to me. For the remaining nine areas where decisions were announced in July 2026, we will consider these areas further in the next phase of the review. More detail on these areas can be found in the Written Statement I will make to Parliament today. Next phases of the review: timetable and next steps I now turn to the next phase of the review, which will focus on the following areas due to have mindedto decisions, in no particular order: • the four areas (Essex, Southend-on-Sea and Thurrock; Hampshire, Isle of Wight, Portsmouth and Southampton; Norfolk; and Suffolk) where decisions were withdrawn in September; and • the two areas (West Sussex and Cambridgeshire and Peterborough) where decisions have not yet been taken. • the nine areas where decisions have been today withdrawn. I am mindful that you want clarity and I will work as fast as I can with officials to provide that clarity. As I have said, I believe it is right to take a structured approach to the review and give each area the serious and deep consideration it deserves given the significance of these decisions. We will, however, seek to inform councils at the earliest practical opportunity, meaning that some of these 15 remaining areas will receive an outcome before others. We commit to providing updates on outcomes in another set of areas by Christmas – and that every area will have an outcome announced by early next year. As previously communicated in September, we set out that the review meant that elections scheduled to take place in May 2027 would go ahead to existing councils. This remains the position for the areas covered by the review. In those areas, the timetable is elections to new councils in May 2028 followed by vesting day on 1 April 2029. While I appreciate that this revised timetable will have implications for local planning and implementation activity, it provides the time needed both to complete the review and to ensure that any subsequent implementation programme can be delivered successfully.
