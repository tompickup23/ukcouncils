As previously communicated in September, we set out that the review meant that elections scheduled to take place in May 2027 would go ahead to existing councils. This remains the position for the areas covered by the review. In those areas, the timetable is elections to new councils in May 2028 followed by vesting day on 1 April 2029. While I appreciate that this revised timetable will have implications for local planning and implementation activity, it provides the time needed both to complete the review and to ensure that any subsequent implementation programme can be delivered successfully. Previously announced funding I recognise the impact and costs of ongoing uncertainty and delay, which I have also considered in the initial phase of the review. I understand that areas will have incurred costs associated with local government reorganisation in good faith. For areas where new unitaries were previously announced, we will pay 50% of the £900,000 transition support per new unitary, based on the number of new unitaries in the initial announcement. This funding is guaranteed and will be paid in the coming weeks. It is not contingent on the outcome of the Review. For Cambridgeshire and Peterborough and West Sussex, where no decisions have been previously announced, we will instead pay £500,000 to each area. When final decisions are made, we will pay the balance of the £900,000 transition support per each new unitary, taking account of funding already paid to its area, as well as £150,000 of supplementary funding per new unitary for Leadership Capacity and Continuity in Children’s Services, Adult Social Care and Public Health. Next steps Ministers and officials will remain in regular contact with councils while further consideration continues, providing updates as decisions are taken. I have also set out further detail in the Written Ministerial Statement on important elements such as city status and ceremonial matters and the transition away from the Committee system. My officials are available to discuss any questions you have on those. I am copying this letter to your Chief Executives, MPs, and relevant Commissioners and Combined Authority officials. Thank you again for your continued commitment, leadership and dedication to serving your residents and communities. Yours sincerely, JIM MCMAHON OBE MP Minister for Local Government, Devolution and Regional Growth
