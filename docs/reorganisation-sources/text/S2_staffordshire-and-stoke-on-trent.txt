Timeline 5 February 2025 The invitation letter sent to councils in Staffordshire and Stoke-on-Trent set out their milestones for reorganisation. They were invited to submit final proposals for unitary local government by 28 November 2025. 5 February 2026 ​A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament. This set out that a statutory consultation on the proposals received for local government reorganisation in Staffordshire and Stoke-on-Trent was launched. On the same day, the Minister of State for Local Government and Homelessness, Alison McGovern MP, wrote to the leaders of councils in Staffordshire and Stoke-on-Trent to thank them for their proposals and to inform them of the consultation. 16 July 2026 ​A written ministerial statement was made to Parliament, setting out the Secretary of State’s decision to implement the proposal for 2 unitary councils to be established in Staffordshire and Stoke-on-Trent. ​Secondary legislation, which will be subject to Parliamentary approval, will now be taken forward to abolish existing councils, establish new councils and make transitional arrangements. ​Accompanying this announcement, letters were sent to leaders in Staffordshire and Stoke-on-Trent .
