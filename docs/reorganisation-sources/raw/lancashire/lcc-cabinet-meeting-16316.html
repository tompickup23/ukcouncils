Consultation on Park and Ride Sites PDF 152 KB

Additional documents:

  • Appendix A , item 5. PDF 187 KB View as DOCX (5./2) 414 KB

Minutes:

Cabinet considered a report that sought to undertake a public consultation and stakeholder engagement on proposals regarding the future closure of Lancashire County Council's Park and Ride facilities at Walton-le-Dale, Preston, and Caton Road (M6 Junction 34), Lancaster. It was noted that the consultation would consider the likely impacts, available alternative travel options and any measures needed to mitigate concerns raised to inform a subsequent Cabinet report containing recommendations and a final decision on the two park and ride sites.

 

Resolved: That

 

i.  The commencement of public consultation and stakeholder engagement on the potential cessation of Park and Ride services operating from Walton-le-Dale, Preston, and Caton Road (M6 Junction 34), Lancaster be approved;

ii.  Officers be authorised to undertake all necessary consultation activities required under the Lancashire County Combined Authority Bus Service Improvement Plan Enhanced Partnership arrangements; and

iii.  Officers be asked to prepare a further report for Cabinet setting out the outcome of the consultation, Equality Impact Assessment findings, financial implications and recommendations regarding the future of Park and Ride provision in Lancashire.
