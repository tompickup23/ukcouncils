Models of Care - Better Care, Better Lives PDF 211 KB

  • View item 6. as DOCX 143 KB
  • View the background to item 6.

Additional documents:

  • Appendix A , item 6. PDF 1 MB
  • Appendix B , item 6. PDF 4 MB
  • Appendix C , item 6. PDF 296 KB View as DOCX (6./4) 171 KB

Minutes:

Cabinet considered a report that presented Lancashire’s future Model of Care for Adult Social Care, which built on the strategic direction endorsed by Cabinet in May 2026. It was noted that the model had been shaped through extensive co-production and engagement with people who drew on care and support, unpaid carers, staff, providers, communities, and partners. It provided the blueprint for the future of Adult Social Care in Lancashire and set out how care and support would be organised around people's lives, local communities, and earlier support, with the county council playing a clear anchoring role in shaping provision across Lancashire.

 

Cabinet was asked to endorse the model as the strategic approach for the future delivery of Adult Social Care. It was noted that detailed design and implementation planning would continue through co-production, but that the direction was clear: a community-led, person-centred, and sustainable model of care that supported people to live good lives in the places they called home.

 

It was also highlighted that the report did not seek approval for any specific service or building changes or individual investment decisions.

 

Resolved: That

 

i.  Lancashire’s Model of Care be agreed as the strategic model for Adult Social Care; and

ii.  That detailed design and implementation planning be progressed through co-production with residents, carers, communities, providers, and partners.

Cottam Parkway Railway Station Project – Capital Programme PDF 178 KB

  • View item 11. as DOCX 130 KB
  • View the background to item 11.

Minutes:

Cabinet considered a report that sought approval to add the Cottam Parkway Railway Station Project to Lancashire County Council's Capital Programme to enable delivery of a £69.2m major transport infrastructure scheme. It was noted that the project was predominantly externally funded, with £64.7m provided through the Department for Transport, Lancashire Combined County Authority and other partner contributions, while £4.5m was funded by Lancashire County Council. The county council was to act as the scheme promoter and delivery body and would be responsible for procuring and delivering elements of the project on behalf of the funding partners. Approval was also sought for the delivery and governance arrangements required to facilitate project delivery.

 

Resolved: That, the following be approved:

 

i.  The inclusion of the Cottam Parkway Railway Station Project within the Lancashire County Council Capital Programme, subject to final confirmation of Department for Transport and Lancashire Combined County Authority funding and associated grant conditions;

ii.  A capital budget of £69.2m for the delivery of the project, which £64.7m would be funded from approved external partner contributions and £4.5m from previously approved county council funding sources;

iii.  The continued delivery of the station and car park elements of the project through Network Rail in accordance with Cabinet's decision of 8 September 2022 to directly award the railway-related elements of the scheme to Network Rail;

iv.  The procurement of the highway and bridge works package by Lancashire County Council in accordance with the county council's Procurement Rules and applicable procurement legislation; and

v.  The Executive Director of Place be authorised in consultation with the Cabinet Member for Highways and Transport, the Corporate Director of Finance and the Director of Law and Governance, to take all actions necessary to deliver the Cottam Parkway Railway Station Project within the approved budget, including undertaking procurement activities, awarding contracts, and entering into and completing funding, land, property and delivery agreements.

Local Government Reorganisation: Review and Preservation of the Council's Position PDF 163 KB

  • View item 13a as DOCX 139 KB
  • View the background to item 13a

Additional documents:

  • Appendix A , item 13a PDF 205 KB
  • Appendix B , item 13a PDF 155 KB
  • Appendix C , item 13a PDF 168 KB
  • Appendix D , item 13a PDF 158 KB

Minutes:

Cabinet considered a report that provided an update on the Government's announcement of 7 September 2026 that local government reorganisation affecting Lancashire, Blackburn with Darwen and Blackpool had been paused and was under review; and on the council's subsequent correspondence with Government.

 

It was noted that the Government's decision had significant implications for Lancashire’s residents, local services and public finances. The information provided by Government at that time had not sufficiently explained the comparative assessment and evidence underlying the decision, and the timing of the report had reflected the urgent need to consider preserving the Council’s position while continuing to seek the fuller reasons and information required to understand and assess the decision.

 

In the absence of satisfactory confirmation that the decision of 16 July 2026 had been withdrawn and that a fresh decision would be issued following the review, authority was sought to commence protective proceedings to preserve the Council's position, with a request that the proceedings be stayed until the outcome of the Government's review.

 

It was noted that the reason for urgency was due to any protective claim having to be filed promptly and in any event by 16 October 2026 at the latest.

 

Resolved: That

 

i.  The Government's review and pause of local government reorganisation, the Council's request for clarification, and that no substantive response had been received that confirmed that the decision of 16 July 2026 be withdrawn and a fresh decision following the review provided, including sufficient reasons and information to enable the Council to understand how the decision was reached by reference to the Government’s published criteria, be noted;

ii.  The Director of Law and Governance be authorised, in consultation with the Leader and the Deputy Chief Executive and Section 151 Officer, to take such legal steps as were considered necessary and proportionate to protect the Council's position. Those steps may include taking no immediate action, seeking further clarification, commencing protective proceedings and seeking a stay, or taking any associated procedural step, including discontinuing or withdrawing proceedings where appropriate;

iii.  It be noted that a further update report would be brought to Cabinet when the information was available on the outcome of the Government’s review for Lancashire with options provided on next steps including the continuation of any protective proceedings or withdrawal; and

iv.  The decision be implemented immediately for the purposes of Standing Order C28(3) as any delay could adversely affect the execution of the county council's responsibilities. The reason for this was that any protective claim must be filed promptly and in any event by 16 October 2026 at the latest.
