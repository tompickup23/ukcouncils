# UK Councils release work, 6 October 2026

## Item 1: contextual links

Prepared on `feat/tier-safe-contextual-links` from main `3a73223`.
The assessment and section 4 are the authority for this release.
The existing local guide and planning edits are preserved and excluded.

Removed the network promotional footer, tier C destinations and the payments
explorer links. AI DOGE remains named as the payments source system on the
methodology page. Council records link to place, population, elections and
education-authority calendars in their relevant sections. Tax links remain on
the live UK Councils hub until the redirect map has been reviewed.

`node scripts/check-context-links.mjs` verifies HTTP status and council identity
from the existing GSS mappings and UK Places sitemap. It recorded 1,020 verified
links from 1,021 candidates. Surrey Heath's Elections mapping returns a page
headed Surrey, so it is withheld. The calendar identity is the education
authority, explicitly including a district's parent. The evidence is in
`data/links/checks.json`; only passed links enter `data/links/contextual.json`.
Normalised titles are evidence fields, not copy rendered on the website.

The UK State footer feed could not be fetched. Local About, Contact, Corrections
and Privacy remain. Builds try the optional feed once, with a five-second
limit. The supported manifest fields are `method` and `corrections`, both HTTPS
URLs on ukstate.co.uk. Missing or unsupported manifests retain the local footer.
Confirm the contract when the network feed is published.

The no-download release gate also required removal of existing council, table
and comparison CSV controls. Sharing and printing remain. Source publications
remain linked for provenance. No source figures were changed.

Validation and deployment: pending.
